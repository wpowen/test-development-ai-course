# TD-F05 Research Brief

## Controlling question

What is the task, population, error cost, independent oracle, and decision before choosing F1, Recall@k, ROUGE, latency, QPS, or a business KPI? The source document names common AI tasks and quality dimensions; it does not provide a universal threshold. This topic turns that knowledge into a Metric Card that a beginner can fill and an expert can challenge.

The scope includes classification, ranking, generation, RAG, Agent, serving, performance, robustness, safety, usability, explainability, and business slices. It excludes a production threshold without a denominator, version, owner, risk slice, uncertainty, and failure action. AI may suggest candidate metrics, but an independent oracle and a named quality owner decide.

## Evidence / Inference / Unknown

Evidence: official metrics and professional guidance distinguish service signals, quality outcomes, risk, and governance. Inference: task-to-oracle-to-metric sequencing reduces misuse of universal numbers. Unknown: actual business loss function, population distribution, release threshold, and regulatory context.

## Learner decision

The learner must complete one Metric Card, state what the metric can and cannot prove, and run TD-F05 baseline/fault/repair. Missing denominator or oracle is a blocker, not a prompt for a guessed value.
