# TD-C04 Evidence Synthesis

## Fact

The user career document contains a detailed P5–P9 matrix, experience ranges, responsibility descriptions, and threshold-like numbers. This is valuable user-provided input but not an authenticated employer policy: its organization, author, sample, denominator, and effective date are unknown. Governance and reliability references support explicit owners, versions, review cycles, and action boundaries; they do not define a job band.

The safe fact for the course is therefore that an organization adapter needs a source reference, owner, version, effective date, mapping rule, threshold owner, and review cycle. Until those fields are supplied by the target organization, the result is `INTERNAL-UNKNOWN/BLOCK`.

## Cross-source synthesis

Route A preserves the source matrix as candidate content and teaches how responsibility expands with scope, failure cost, and technical influence. Route B injects false certainty: page completion becomes P6, years become seniority, an expired policy remains active, or an owner is omitted. Both routes agree that a generic curriculum cannot approve an internal title. Adjudication: map public responsibility states to organization bands only through a versioned adapter; otherwise retain UNKNOWN.

The method is: public state → organization source → owner approval → effective version → role mapping → Metric Card → review cycle. Prompt output can format an adapter but cannot populate missing policy fields. The runner mutates status, failure action, and guess level to prove the block is observable.

## Inference

Separating a public responsibility ladder from an internal adapter preserves the useful career narrative while avoiding false portability. It also makes policy drift explicit: a superseded policy must stop mapping until a new owner-approved version exists.

## Unknown

Target organization, HR/engineering policy, band names, compensation, promotion evidence, effective dates, thresholds, reviewer authority, and hiring outcomes remain unknown. No P5–P9 claim is made by this course.
