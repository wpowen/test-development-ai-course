# TD-C04 Research Brief

## Controlling question

Which organization-owned evidence is sufficient to map a public responsibility state to an internal band, title, or threshold? The user document contains a useful P5–P9 matrix and experience ranges, but the organization, policy owner, version, effective date, and denominator are not supplied. A course must teach the adapter method while refusing to guess the learner's level.

The scope covers source reference, policy owner, version, effective date, mapping rule, threshold owner, review interval, and the fail-closed `INTERNAL-UNKNOWN/BLOCK` outcome. It excludes any real HR policy, promotion decision, salary inference, or employment promise. AI can format an adapter candidate, but it cannot infer a band from page completion, years, or tool count.

## Evidence / Inference / Unknown

Evidence: the source provides a concrete career matrix; governance and reliability guidance require explicit ownership and review. Inference: an organization adapter preserves the useful matrix without turning it into universal truth. Unknown: every target organization's bands, policy language, and approval path.

## Learner decision

The learner must either attach policy evidence and a named owner or leave the mapping unknown. Run TD-C04 baseline/fault/repair and never change `expected` to make a guessed level pass.
