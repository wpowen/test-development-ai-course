# TD-C02 Independent Comparison

## Agreements

Route A (profession and standards) and Route B (failure-first and reuse) agree that responsibility must be observable, owned, and reviewable. Both require an artifact, evidence references, an independent oracle, and a named human decision. Both reject years, title, tool count, generated prose, or a single green report as sufficient proof.

## Disagreements

Route A can make a generic ladder look authoritative because it emphasizes shared competency and governance language. Route B warns that a ladder without policy context can become a hidden promotion rubric and that a missing reviewer can make a confident map meaningless. The user source is rich on progression but does not identify the target organization or validate fixed weights, years, quotas, or thresholds.
