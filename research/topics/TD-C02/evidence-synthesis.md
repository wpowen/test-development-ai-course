# TD-C02 Evidence Synthesis

## Fact

The opened professional syllabus and governance, reliability, quality, security, and evaluation references support a common pattern: work must have an accountable owner, an observable outcome, a reproducible check, and a review or decision point. They support learning about risk, evidence, controls, and feedback. They do not support a universal P5–P9 definition, a fixed experience threshold, or the claim that one course artifact proves seniority.

The user-provided career document is treated as an adjudicated professional input. It contributes the candidate progression from execution to broader influence, the importance of project complexity, and the idea that quality and technical influence matter. Its authorship, organization, samples, fixed weights, years, quotas, and promotion numbers are not independently established, so those atoms are adapted into a ladder and an organization adapter rather than copied as facts.

## Cross-source synthesis

Route A (profession and standards) emphasizes responsibility, governance, quality attributes, owners, and decision evidence. Route B (failure-first and reuse) asks what happens when evidence references are empty, an implementation output is reused as an oracle, a reviewer is missing, or years are treated as level. The two routes agree that a responsibility claim must be tied to an artifact and an independent reviewer; they differ in whether a generic ladder is sufficient. The adjudication keeps the public four-state ladder but requires organization-specific policy before any band mapping.

The resulting method is: task → responsibility state → decision rights → failure cost → reusable artifact → seeded fault → independent reviewer → escalation or reevaluation. Prompt generation is a candidate step only. The exact-field runner provides a deterministic baseline, mutates evidence/years-only fields, and repairs the known-good observation. This is a fixture contract, not a live career assessment.

## Inference

An evidence ladder should be more reusable than a title list because a learner can attach a file, command, failure, reviewer, and decision to each state. A state becomes stronger when the learner can explain risk, choose or challenge an oracle, preserve a failure boundary, and hand the result to another owner. This is an engineering inference from the source and method evidence, not an industry-wide promotion rule.
