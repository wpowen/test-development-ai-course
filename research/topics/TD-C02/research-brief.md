# TD-C02 Research Brief

## Controlling question

How can a beginner prove progression from guided execution to independent scoped ownership, cross-team leverage, and strategy governance without using years or titles as a proxy? The user-provided career document supplies a progression candidate, but its employer, sample, and policy context are unknown. This topic therefore treats responsibility as a public evidence contract and treats P5–P9 mapping as an organization-owned adapter.

The research scope covers observable work, decision rights, failure cost, reusable artifact, independent oracle, reviewer, and escalation. It excludes a real promotion decision, salary claim, hiring promise, and any assertion that completing a fixture proves professional seniority. AI may extract candidate evidence and draft a map; a named human owner must accept the responsibility boundary.

## Evidence / Inference / Unknown

Evidence: standards and testing guidance describe accountability, measurable outcomes, risk ownership, and repeatable verification. Inference: a four-state ladder makes the source career narrative teachable and portable when every state has an artifact and reviewer. Unknown: whether a target organization uses the same names, thresholds, or decision rights.

## Learner decision

The learner must decide which responsibility state is supported by current evidence, which decision remains escalated, and which next artifact will create stronger evidence. Empty references remain `UNKNOWN`; the first command is the TD-C02 baseline/fault/repair cycle.
