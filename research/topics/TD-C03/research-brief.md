# TD-C03 Research Brief

## Controlling question

How can a beginner convert “I want to learn AI testing” into claims, evidence references, gap routes, and a 30/60/90-day artifact plan? The career source provides staged learning, skills, projects, and assessment ideas; it does not provide this learner's starting level or prove learning effectiveness. The course therefore records evidence or `UNKNOWN` and routes each gap to a page, exercise, owner, and review date.

The scope includes traditional testing baseline, AI task literacy, evaluation practice, responsibility state, evidence references, a 30-day foundation artifact, a 60-day seeded-fault receipt, and a 90-day independent review. It excludes employment, promotion, salary, certification, and causal claims about one learner's outcome. AI may draft routes, but a human reviewer must inspect each claim and boundary.

## Evidence / Inference / Unknown

Evidence: professional guidance supports measurable outcomes, owners, feedback, and repeatable evaluation. Inference: milestone artifacts are more reusable than an unqualified checklist. Unknown: learner motivation, available time, mentor quality, and transfer to a real team.

## Learner decision

The learner must identify the next artifact that closes one gap, name the reviewer, and preserve any unproven claim as `UNKNOWN`. Run TD-C03 baseline/fault/repair before adapting the plan.
