# TD-C03 Evidence Synthesis

## Fact

Opened professional and technical references consistently favor explicit outcomes, owners, feedback, repeatable evaluation, risk-aware evidence, and a distinction between tool capability and actual result. They support a staged learning contract and reviewable artifacts. They do not prove that a learner will finish a plan, obtain a job, reach a band, or produce a production-quality system.

The user career document contributes a broad sequence: traditional testing baseline, AI/ML and LLM foundations, task-specific evaluation, engineering practice, collaboration, projects, self-assessment, and staged growth. The source does not supply a personal baseline, mentor, available time, or causal learning sample, so the course turns its route into claims, evidence references, gap routes, and review dates.

## Cross-source synthesis

Route A (standards and learning outcomes) asks whether a capability has a clear outcome, owner, practice, and review. Route B (failure-first and transfer) tests empty evidence references, a plan with no executable artifact, a milestone that promises promotion, and a route that cannot survive a background change. Agreement: a claim with no evidence is `UNKNOWN`; a plan without a next artifact is not actionable. Adjudication: the learner produces a 30-day foundation artifact, a 60-day seeded-fault receipt, and a 90-day independent review, while preserving unknowns.

The method is: current evidence → capability claim → evidence-ref check → maturity boundary → gap route → 30-day artifact → 60-day fault evidence → 90-day review → boundary statement. Prompt output must match the schema and cannot invent a reviewer or outcome. The runner mutates evidence, unknown, and plan fields, so a false PASS becomes a visible FAIL.

## Inference

Routing a gap to one page, one practice, and one reviewer should reduce the ambiguity of a generic learning list. The 30/60/90 cadence is a planning adapter, not a guarantee; its value must be checked by review receipts and transfer quality, not calendar completion alone.

## Unknown

Individual background, time, mentor availability, learning effectiveness, employer expectations, salary, hiring demand, and promotion criteria remain unknown. These cannot be inferred from fixture completion or an AI-generated self-assessment.
