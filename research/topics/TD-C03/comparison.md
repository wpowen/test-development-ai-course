# TD-C03 Independent Comparison

## Agreements

Both research routes agree that learning claims need an observable outcome, a reviewable artifact, a named reviewer, and a clear boundary. Both reject a checklist of technologies, empty evidence references, and an AI-generated PASS as proof of mastery.

## Disagreements

Route A emphasizes staged competency and measurable learning outcomes; Route B emphasizes missing evidence, false certainty, time constraints, and background transfer. A calendar can organize work but cannot prove that a learner performed it. A broad course map can guide a beginner but cannot replace an individual baseline or mentor review.
